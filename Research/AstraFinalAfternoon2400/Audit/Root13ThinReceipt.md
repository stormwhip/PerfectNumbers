The two new exclusions are precisely 535 and 536; the previously verified 537 through 618 remain the older segment of this same condition. This does not bound the entire nonlinear Euler sigma contribution when a high Euler exponent is possible at smaller linear valuation.

[Thin618](../ElevenThirteen/Thin618.py) independently cold-reconstructs the possible emitter shapes, not a supplied list: at 536 only `(h,c)=(10,5)`; at 535 `(8,3)` and `(10,5)`, where the required ordinary emitter prime lies in `A=σ(13^618)` with exact valuation `c` in `A`. The preceding [HighLinear proof and review](Root13HighLinearReceipt.md) gives Euler exteriority, exponent one, internal emitter and primitive odd order for these high windows; the strict entry comparisons here also hold at s=535. No assumed squarefreeness or guessed factorization. Congruence forces `q=2mY−1`, `m` in the checked odd progression and canonical bans apply. From any lower endpoint `Q≤q`, the exact power lower bound on the emitter and the distinct mass constraint produce shrinking `lo,hi` bounds (`lo` increases and `hi` decreases with `Q`). Both side bounds on the nonzero signed multiple of `Y` are non-increasing thereafter, so checking strict obstruction at the cutoff covers the entire infinite multiplier tail. Lower multipliers are all checked for compositeness, never certified by a probable-prime pass. Positive and negative residues are reconstructed directly in [CheckThin618](CheckThin618.py) and compared via cross-products.
