# Strict masked pivot exclusions at (19,432)

This is a conditional certificate for odd perfect `N ≤ 10^2400` with `19^432 ∥ N`. This does not by itself close the frozen shard [P19_I00082](../../FinalSprint/Delta.json#L786-L797). No ledger is changed.

```powershell
python -I -S -B Research/Night2400/G2/Verify.py
```

The [checker](Verify.py) requires only standard-library Python, [the packet](Nineteen432.json.gz), and the frozen public source tree. Each forest is computed both by the [Compose integer recurrence](../../Nov25/Compose/Compose.py) and a second one-Euler integer recurrence that independently computes legal sigma emissions. Unexplored indices are covered by the monotone component-size lower bounds. The checker requires exact bounds strictly greater than `10^2400` and independently checks incoming valuations, graph ownership, and every certified prime label. A composite sigma remainder is never assumed to be prime.

The canonical support order is `(19,7,11,31,13,3,5,17)`. There are 128 masks containing 19, 55 in the frozen [support projection](../../Session20261126/OpenSupport.json). The checker recomputes each first split at the prime `Q=11842464881317660217`, which has unit valuation in `σ(19^432)`. It checks all positive `f=v_Q(N)` with `19^432(σ(19^432)/Q)Q^f ≤ 10^2400`; the first omitted value is 69. Exact arithmetic leaves the 26 valuations `1,2,4,5,6,8,9,10,12,13,14,16,17,18,20,21,22,24,25,26,28,29,30,32,33,34` in the raw union, totaling 2440 mask-exponent pairs (1006 in projection). Fixed-root minimum abundancy excludes 40 of the 128 masks independently of `f`, all outside the projection.

The packet proves 625 mask–pivot leaves (555 ordinary forest leaves, one forced-Euler forest leaf, and 69 branch abundance leaves) on the 88 masks not already excluded by fixed-root abundancy. Its SHA-256 is `9fa08422b67fd1d09bdcbbedd7d61511539e700cf0907adc13ee22dfeaf6ae95`. The completely excluded pivot valuations are `2,5,9,13,17,21,25,29,32,33,34`; exact valuation 2 is excluded in every one of the 88 masks. The projected masks `67,81,113,189,197,223` are excluded in full, including all pivot siblings and the monotone tail. This leaves 908 compatible cells on 82 masks before applying additional historical support restrictions, or 584 cells on the 49 remaining projected masks.

The lowest odd pivot branch is hard: `σ(Q)=2·3²·29·22686714332026169`, fixing Q as Euler. The raw prefix floor is 1123 and ordinary discovery on nine selected masks of this fiber reached a maximum floor of 2117. A joint valuation test targeting both fixed bases did not get over the high-Euler inequality in a representative low mask. In contrast `σ(Q²)=727·9283·1475563·2910634680649·4838558467621` is completely factored and enables a much stronger low-even forest. Root `σ(19^432)/Q` remains composite and unsplit in the bounded discovery trial.

At mask 113 and `v_Q=16`, the presence of 5 and absence of 7 rule out exponents 2, 4, 5 for 5; exponents 3 and all other odd values below 6 except 1 fail Euler parity. Exact minimum abundancy is at least `2.068765` on the whole `v_5≥6` tail, so `5^1 ∥ N` and the Euler slot is fixed. Both forest implementations then give a strict cutoff for the exact three-component prefix (floor at least 2401 in the untrimmed discovery test). [Special.py](Special.py) optionally regenerates the discovery graph for this leaf.
