# Root three: finite source windows and support-refined exact trees

For a putative odd perfect number `N <= 10^2400`, rooted at `3^e || N`, primes `7,11,13,19,31` are absent. The Euler prime `q` and its exponent `a` are both `1 mod 4`. Write `s=v3(sigma(q^a))`, `Y=3^s`. A window is an exact finite subset of `{0,...,e}`; no unresolved cell is a proof.

## Root sieve

Put `P=3^(e+1)`, `A=(P-1)/2` and trial strip all primes through 100000. Check extra certified factors against A. For each known `z^c || A` charge the minimum permissible even floor `m>=c` (no forbidden prime divides `sigma(z^m)`). All primes used by a release must pass deterministic MR below `2^64` or the published ECPP checker above it. In an unconditional test delete the entire largest admissible Euler premium. In the Euler-one case with `s>0`, quadratic reciprocity implies `q` does not divide `A`; for high Euler and `Y>1+floor(log_5 B)`, `q` is also outside `A`: a divisor q of A has `(3/q)=1` and therefore `q=1 mod 3`, implying `Y | a+1` by LTE.

For a non-Euler supplier `z=1 mod 3`, supplying j units requires `f>=3^j-1`, and `3^j|(f+1)`. Thus if a threshold is forbidden, all higher thresholds are forbidden; the minimum required exponent advances in nested steps. For known z, grant all units through its floor and sort incremental prices. (Keeping its old uncharged prices as additional slots weakens the bound.) For unknown prime `z` in a coprime residual cyclotomic block with index h, `ord_z(3)=h` since `z>T>h`; hence a supplier is `1 mod lcm(2h,3)`. Exclude candidate divisors through the stated cutoff by trial `U % z != 0` without a primality assumption. Let `L` denote a resulting lower bound. Free units require at least `z^2` per unit already paid. Grant `floor(log_(L^2)(U))` free units and `floor(log_L(U))` slots at price L. This is conservative: each prime can have **only one** strictly positive exponent increment of size 1 beyond its prepaid extent; after that all increments are >=2. All unaccounted slots cost at least `(T+1)^2`. Independently insert known ordinary costs and cap every marginal at `(T+1)^2`. The root upper `high=max(5^5,3^(5s-5))` used as a lower bound on `q^a` for a>=5 is the same as the upstream Euler-source verifier.

For `a=1,s>0`, `Q=2Y-1 <= q`. Some even source `r^f` not the root emits q. When `8*3^e*Q^4 >= 27B`, `3` does not divide `f+1`: removing all 3 factors from `f+1` preserves the q divisibility, by order modulo q, and otherwise gives `r^f>(2q/3)^3`. Therefore that source supplies zero valuation and can be removed without debiting any of the valuation charge. An external source yields `r^f>2q/3`. A source in A with `cA=v_r(A)` yields `r^(f-cA)> qY/[max(P,q)(P/Y+4)]` by the balanced D,E lemma below. A known source left undecided can be tested at *every* integer even f through the exact size upper bound: `sigma(r^f)=qw` implies `w= -sigma(r^f) mod Y` and `w<=sigma(r^f)/Q`. No assumed factorization of sigma is needed for this step.

**Balanced lemma (restated).** Write `u=(P-1)/r^cA`, `t=r^(f-cA)`, `w=sigma(r^f)/q`, `H=max(1,P/q)`, `delta=P/Y`. The integers `D=uw(r-1)-u-rt` and `E=u(w+sigma(r^(cA-1)))-sigma(r^(f-cA))` are both multiples of Y. D is nonzero since `D=-P mod r-1`; E is nonzero since `r^cA E=P(w+sigma(r^(cA-1)))-w(q+1)` and `s+v3(w)<=e`. Also `uw<3Ht/2`. If `r-1<delta+2`, then for positive D, `D< Htr`; for negative D, `-D< (3H/2+r)t`; both are < `(delta+4)Ht` since `r<=delta+2`. If `r-1>=delta+2`, negative E has `-E<3t/2`, and positive E has `Y<=E<3Ht/2+P/(r-1)<=3Ht/2+delta Y/(delta+2)`. Each case gives `t>Y/[(delta+4)H]`. Replacing q by Q in `q^2/max(P,q)` is conservative.

## Tree

For exact components let X be their product and S their sigma product, D=`S/gcd(S,2X)`. Then `XD | N`. Reject oversize, abundancy `S>2X`, and divisibility of D by an assigned or forbidden prime. Strip D using certified pool (includes every allowed prime <2000). Let U be its rough part. If q is a known factor of D it needs valuation 1 and `q+1=0 mod Y`; a feasible case stops this version. For q in U, the cofactor test on `gcd(U,sigma_i)` for each exact component must exclude it: `v=q h` implies `h=-v mod Y` with `0<h<=v/Q`. The root cannot emit q. Thus q is external to D and its minimum Q multiplies the full D even floor without overlap. Repeat marginal pricing for the remaining `e-s-v3(S)` units, with conservative rough minimum 2000. Branch on the largest known unassigned r; enumerate ALL even legal f up to the room allowed by the smallest Q among live windows. Children use `X*r^f`, `S*sigma(r^f)` with strict actual multiplicity, not lcm. Unknown secondary edges are freely omitted; provided ones require exact divisibility plus certified primality. Discovery caps/depth cannot close a branch.

## Support-refined source rule (BatchS and other boost packets)

If q cannot divide S, the source is **unassigned**. If `r` lies outside D, it costs `r^f>2q/3` beyond the floor. For known `r^c || D` with charged minimum `m`, the overlap of this bound is just `r^m`. If `cA>0`, the balanced lemma counts over `r^cA`, so the only loss is `r^(m-cA)`. For any remaining case use the finite sigma cofactor check above. In all cases other suppliers' lower bound still holds: our price table grants extra slots to *all* forced primes, including the zero-supply source.

For an unknown r, split U into pairwise-coprime supported blocks by gcd with each individual sigma, taking the *entire supported part* (iterated gcd or gcd with successive squares). For a block v, `r^c<=v`. If `g=gcd(A,v)>1`, refinement by A ensures **every prime in this block divides A**. Therefore `r^(c-cA)<=v/g` whenever c>=cA (and the bound is safe for c<cA too); debit only `v/g` in the balanced comparison. This argument does not treat a rough composite as prime. Check product and gcd partition independently, or test by the constructive splitter.
