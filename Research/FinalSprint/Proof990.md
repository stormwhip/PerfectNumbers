## Two simultaneous valuation prices

If p^h is ordinary (even h), let k3,k5 be the 3,5 valuations of σ(p^h). For l=3,5, a positive k_l requires p≡1 (mod l) since no nontrivial odd order divides l−1. Then LTE gives k_l=v_l(h+1). Consequently 3^k3·5^k5 divides h+1 and h≥2k3+4k5. Each 3-supplier p≥43: the only non-banned possibility below 43 is 37, and 7 divides Φ3(37), which divides σ(37^h) whenever k3>0. Every 5-supplier p≥41. Thus net(p,h)=h ln p−2 k3 ln43−4 k5 ln41 ≥0 for *each allowed ordinary prime*, even when p<43 or <41 (corresponding supplies necessarily zero). Universal caps k3≤6 and k5≤4 follow from 43^(3^7−1)>B and 41^(5^5−1)>B. In particular _no negative subtree refund_ is possible; this is essential for pruning a parent prefix or stopping a partial child sum. All charged explicit prices are checked under the specific (43,41). If applied elsewhere this nonnegativity must be re-proved or refunds reserved.

Set u=v5((q+1)/2), 0≤u≤d. Write b5=v5(σ(r^f)); if u>0 then q≡−1 (mod 5) implies 5∤q−1, whereas f+1=ord_q(r) divides q−1, so 5∤f+1, hence b5=0. If u=0 then b5≤4. Let D=max(0,d−u−4·[u=0]); at least D units of ordinary non-emitter 5-supply are necessary; A has no 5 factor. Write U=e−s, all from ordinary non-emitter components. For volume costing, allocate U three units and any D of the available five units: i and j of these respectively come from primes on A excluding r. We can select a subset of the five units so that 0≤j≤D even if the actual excess is larger. The product on internal suppliers is ≥R^(2i+4j), and the external suppliers' compulsory cost is ≥2(U−i)ln43+4(D−j)ln41, *besides* the net cost of explicitly priced external components. The net defined with true supplies always underestimates the net under a subset allocation. There is no subtraction for the root 3 or emitter r a second time.

Let q0=(2·3^s−1)5^u≤q and E=2q0/3. Off A, r^f>E. On A let c=v_r(A), t=r^(f−c), Y=3^s, δ=P/Y. The frozen D1/D2 lemma in `Documentation/Proof2400.md` and `Research/Session20261126/NextBase/Internal.md` yields t≥I=max(1,Y·min(1,q0/P)/K), K=3 if R≥2δ+1, else δ+4. The improved K=3 uses r−1≥2δ in D2's positive branch and D2∈Y·Z, D2≠0; it is not a special fact about exponent 1108. Every ordinary power on A is even; A nonsquare therefore forces their full product H≥AR (an odd A multiplicity has to be lifted). External source: q3^e times the extra E times H, H≥max(AR,R^(2i+4j)). Internal source: r|A, the same support must satisfy three bounds H≥AR, H≥AI (pay the additional r^(f−c)), and H≥max(I,E)R^(2i+4j) (internal suppliers excluding r are *disjoint from r*). Use the MAXIMUM, never product, of these overlapping lower bounds. If q|σ(t^h) for more than one component, this would actually violate v_q(2N)=1; no such uniqueness is needed in our partition. These are exactly the two possible positions of the chosen r relative to A.

Each branch is minimized over the full integers s=813..990 and u=0..d. The inner problem over i,j is relaxed to reals: let ‘capacity’ be the difference between the two log hulls, H0−F≥0 (or zero if F larger). Each i,j consumes 2 lnR,4 lnR and saves tariff 2ln43,4ln41 respectively up to U,D. Beyond capacity, savings can never exceed the new cost of raising the second hull, since R>43. Therefore greedily exhaust available volume using greatest saving/weight first (at most two items), then the cheaper item. This relaxation returns a LOWER bound. `fast.py` applies it to integer lower enclosures of both hulls, prices and sizes; ceiling in saved cost (to subtract it) also makes the bound weaker. ## The auxiliary net from 5^d and explained prime chains

5 is external to A, not q (q≥2·3^813−1), and not the chosen emitter. For a tested p∤A, p<Q=2·3^813−1, an E3a source would have b3=0, p^h σ(p^h) A 3^e≤B since Aσ(p^h) divides N and is coprime to 3p (A and σ may overlap each other; multiplicativity ensures their entire product divides 2N). For every even h before this monotone bound fails, require σ(p^h) free of bans and 3, and cofactor w=σ(p^h)/q would satisfy w≡−σ(p^h) mod 3^813. If its least positive residue times Q exceeds σ(p^h), impossible. Rejecting every h certifies p is not the source. `nosource` verifies this independently for 5, all selected external nodes, and every counted external residual prime. In E3b every p outside A is already not r. Named nodes must have p<Q: cannot be Euler q.

One verified prime t|σ(5^d) forces t^{v_t(σ(5^d))}|N. Simple rows add 5^d plus minimum net over all permitted h for each distinct certified t (omit t in E3a if its exclusion as emitter fails). Complete exhaustive prefix, monotone tail h ln t−12ln43−16ln41. For cases below threshold, an acyclic forest has these mandatory primes as roots, unique node per prime. Only when t|σ(p^h) on a *realized* actual exponent activate a named child t owned by p. Demand v_t(σ(p^h)), at least one; incoming demands from other rows are ignored, never miscredited. The verifier takes a minimum over allowed exact h≥demand or an uncrossable tail. Mathematical caps v_l≤6,4 imply the tail bound, even if h improperly large. The initial threshold 560 ln10 plus rounding is a conservative clipping ceiling, *not* a lower-bound assumption; every lawful h with smaller price is enumerated and raises no claim above that clipping. The h-loop covers all affordable h (h<B.bit_length()<8000 since p≥2). For each actual h, account own net plus retained descendants plus a cleaned residue: from σ(p^h) strip 3, every prime of A and every named prime of forest incl. 5 via repeated gcd. Small remaining prime factors p<1000 (excluding 2,3) may receive per-valuation credit max(0,ln p−max(ln43·[p≡1 mod3],ln41·[p≡1 mod5])). This coefficient never exceeds net/h by h≥2k3+4k5 and nonnegative net. In E3a require tested nosource for every such small p. In E3b an unfactored remainder X can additionally be credited ONLY IF 1<X<Q; with all its primes ≥1000 it yields fraction ≥1−ln43/ln1000 of ln X. Thus q cannot occur. E3a leaves all large anonymous parts uncredited, so sharing with the external r is impossible. Product of all emitted selected residuals divides σ(N)=2N, hence all retained odd valuations sum to at most each actual h even across repeated residual factors. Their supports were fully separated from named costs, root, A, source, and q. Conservative omission is allowed even where multiple rows force the same child. The whole auxiliary net is added precisely once to the base; forests replace, rather than add to, the simple child minimum.

## Reproduction

From this package (no paid APIs, network or solver), use Python 3.11+, assertion-enabled, and GCC/Clang w/ unsigned __int128:

```
g++ -O3 -std=c++17 Partial/Base/Trial990.cpp -o Trial990
./Trial990
python -I -S -B prereq.py /path/to/sprint
python -I -S -B check990.py
```

`check990.py` reads no numerical receipts or factor-index databases; its conclusion depends on the stated prerequisite replays as well as the native range. It recomputes all 64+98 alternatives and checks each strictly against the upper threshold 2400·hi(10), including any floor-2400 case.
