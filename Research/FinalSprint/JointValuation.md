# Simultaneous incoming valuation at several fixed components

Let \(B=10^{2400}\), let \(\sigma(N)=2N\) with \(N\) odd and \(N\le B\), and fix distinct certified prime powers \(p_i^{e_i}\parallel N\), \(1\le i\le n\). Set \(X=\prod p_i^{e_i}\), and \(S=\prod \operatorname{odd}(\sigma(p_i^{e_i}))\). Because the sources are distinct, \(S\mid N\). Thus \(c_i=e_i-v_{p_i}(S)\ge0\) and \(C=S/\gcd(S,X)\) is prime to \(X\), with \(XC\mid N\). Put \(M=\prod p_i^{c_i}\). All incoming valuations below are those of odd primes, so passing to the odd part does not change them.

**Joint deficit lemma.** For each nonfixed prime \(r\) consider compatible exponents \(f\ge2\) with \(r^f\le B/X\), and put \(u_i=v_{p_i}(\sigma(r^f))\). For an actual component, \(u_i\le c_i\). Bound its possible deficit above by
\[
D_r=\max(1,\max_f \prod_i p_i^{2u_i}/r^f),\qquad U=\prod_r D_r.
\]
It suffices to overestimate these quantities. If the Euler prime has exponent at least five, or the Euler component has already been fixed, then \(N\ge V=XM^2/U\). If the remaining Euler component is \(q^1\), let \(s_i=v_{p_i}(q+1)\) and \(m=\prod_i p_i^{s_i}\). Then \(N\ge V q/m^2\).

*Proof.* For every \(i\) the nonfixed components contribute exactly \(c_i\) units by multiplicativity. Multiply the actual size of each nonfixed component by its valuation discount. Every term below one costs at most one \(D_r\); no component is charged independently to two budgets. An exponent-one component is factored out rather than estimated by the \(f\ge2\) blanket. ∎

**Exhausting the deficit exceptions.** For a target prime \(p=p_i\) and \(r\ne p\), if \(r\equiv1\pmod p\), LTE gives \(u=v_p(f+1)\). Otherwise positive supply requires \(o=\operatorname{ord}_p(r)>1\) dividing \(\gcd(p-1,f+1)\), and \(u=j+v_p(f+1)\), \(j=v_p(r^o-1)\). The cyclic group modulo \(p\) gives all classes of exact order \(o\); the checker constructs a generator and takes every exponent coprime to \(o\). The derivative \(o r^{o-1}\) is invertible modulo \(p\), so each class has a unique Hensel lift modulo \(p^j\). Enumerate \(1\le j\le c_i-v_p(f+1)\); admitting lifts of greater actual order of divisibility only introduces candidates. The exact truncated valuation is checked before charging.

For \(2\le f<2n\), combine one option per prime by CRT, including a free option for zero supply. For each resulting residue enumerate all translates satisfying \(r^f<\prod p_i^{2u_i}\) using the assumed supply option. The actual tuple occurs, hence this captures every strict deficit. For \(f\ge2n\) a deficit forces \(r^f<p_i^{2n u_i}\) for **at least one** index, by multiplication. Enumerate its one-prime classes and all positive translates under that bound. All exponents beyond \(3^f>B/X\) are impossible. For a candidate r the verifier recomputes every actual \(u_i\). A failed Fermat congruence proves compositeness; a passing candidate is retained even if composite. For each retained base only the largest deficit is required, since its actual exponent is unique. These tests use integer arithmetic and rational products only.

One may choose any nonempty subset of the fixed primes as valuation targets, using its cardinality for the split at $2n$. Omit all other coordinates from $M$, $m$, and the local discounts; retain **all** fixed components in $X,S,C$ and in the interior-block checks. This is the same product identity on fewer coordinates, not an assumption of zero supply at the others.

**Euler-window closure.** In the exponent-one case \(0\le s_i\le c_i\). As \(m\) is odd, \(q\equiv1\pmod4\), and \(m\mid q+1\), we have \(q\equiv Q=2m-1\pmod{4m}\), \(q\ge Q\). If \(VQ>B m^2\), that window is empty. Otherwise, if \(q\nmid C\), then \(XCQ\le N\). If \(q\mid C\), it divides a particular block \(z=\operatorname{odd}(\sigma(p_i^{e_i}))/\gcd(X,\operatorname{odd}(\sigma(p_i^{e_i})))\). Reject the block if \(z<Q\); otherwise its positive cofactor must lie in \(1\le k\le z/Q\) and \(k\equiv zQ^{-1}\pmod{4m}\). Short progressions are checked by exact division. There is also an optional reciprocity rejection: if \(p_i\equiv3\pmod4\) and \(p_i\mid m\), then \(e_i\) is even, and \(q\mid\sigma(p_i^{e_i})\) implies that \(p_i\) has odd order modulo \(q\), hence is a quadratic residue. But \(q\equiv1\pmod4\), \(q\equiv-1\pmod{p_i}\), so reciprocity gives the opposite symbol. All remaining windows must be individually rejected. The joint argument is exact on its fixed path and implies no divisibility lift.

**Implementation and dependencies.** [Integer checker](JointValuation.py), [tree domain and primality checker](JointTree.py); standard Euler form, LTE for odd primes, elementary Hensel lifting, CRT, quadratic reciprocity, and the existing exact forest lemma for non-price siblings. Every internal split is rebuilt from the monotone budget, including the entire tail. The same packet checks the primality of split and leaf labels. No assertion that an anonymous cofactor is squarefree or entirely factored is used.
