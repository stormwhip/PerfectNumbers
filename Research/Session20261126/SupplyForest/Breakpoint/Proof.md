## Split before pricing

Let `A=σ(p^e)`; check prime `t|A`, and write `t^f || N`. Put `c=v_t(A)`. Enumerate **every integer exponent** from `c` until `(p^e A/t^c)t^f>B`, after which none is possible. Disallow incompatible Euler parity and forbidden divisors of `σ(t^f)`. Since distinct component sums multiply into `σ(N)=2N`, `v_p(σ(t^f))<=e` and `A σ(t^f)<=2 p^e t^f` by comparing abundancy of a divisor and N. Put `S=odd(σ(t^f))`, `X=p^e t^f`; `C` is the full `p,t`-coprime part of `A S`, not an assumed complete factorization. Then `X C | N`. If `X C>B`, the branch is dead. Require exactly one frozen row for every still viable exponent and reject missing or extraneous rows.

Check each row's roots divide `C`, carry load `v_z(C)` and are distinct from nonroot labels; these latter divide neither `X` nor `C`. Check graph structure and primality of every label. Set `C0=C/prod z^v_z(C)` over forest roots, `Z=X C prod(nonroots)`. An active vertex `(z,g)` contributes private, support-free remainder `rem` = coprime-to-`Z` part of the odd `σ(z^g)`, plus the powers of each active assigned child. Products of those private remainders divide `N` because valuations from distinct multiplicative components **add**; no pairwise coprimality between remainders is assumed. The baseline lower bound is `X C0 prod_active(z^g rem)` for the actual trace. The mass-only DP permits one Euler location if and only if fixed `f` is even. Otherwise all active exponents are even, since `t` itself occupies the Euler position.

## Valuation ledger with a fixed supplier

Let `k=kappa(p)`, `b=v_p(S)`, `P=max(1,t^f/p^(kb))`, `V=p^((k+1)e) P/U`, where `U` is the pinned Teichmüller non-Euler deficit product. Ordinary labels retain premiums `max(1,z^g/p^(k v_p(σ(z^g))))`; the same component is not included twice. If the Euler prime has exponent ≥5 use blanket divisor `D` exactly as in the parent proof. If `f` is odd then `t` is Euler: for `f≥5` check `V prod(premiums)> B D`, and for `f=1` replace `P` by the exact `t/p^(kb)` (no separate Euler loss). If `f` is even separate three exclusive cases:

1. High Euler anywhere in the active forest or outside: minimum with an optional Euler coupon only allowing `g>=5`, divided by `D`.
2. Euler-one at an active label: use the exact ratio `z/p^(k v_p(z+1))` instead of a clipped premium, with an optional internal coupon; other labels are ordinary. A potentially subunit descendant is accounted for in the tail lower bound. A smaller optional-coupon minimum also safely bounds the case in which the Euler is required to be active.
3. Euler-one exterior to all active labels: ordinary only. Let `v` be its forest premium minimum. For `s=v_p(q+1)`, every `s` with `V v (2p^s-1)>B p^(ks)` is impossible. Let `s0` be the first failure. Cases are paid by the valuation ledger alone if none fails. Otherwise the mixed branch requires `s0>0` and `p=3 mod 4`. Put `Y=p^s0, Q=2Y-1`; thus `q>=Q, q=Q mod 4Y`. Exclude q among labels and fixed `t` by their exact residues. For `q|A`, odd `ord_q(p)` and quadratic reciprocity contradict `q=-1 mod p`. Crucially one must **also** exclude `q|S`: on `n=coprimePart(S,p t)` the cofactor must be positive, congruent to `n Q^(-1) mod 4Y`, and at most `n/Q`. Check its complete finite progression when short; otherwise never declare it excluded.

Now `q` is outside all seeds. Exactly one private emitter may contain q once, because `q||N`. When `rem/q` is possible use its least positive residue mod `4Y` as a lower bound; the emitter coupon is spendable at most once globally. On the **same** trace combine mass to the power `k-1` and valuation to the power one, obtaining `N^k >= (X C0)^(k-1) V (Q/Y)^k * mixed_tree_min`. The price `(Q/Y)^k` is valid uniformly since `q/p^s>=2-p^-s>=Q/Y`. At each ordinary vertex multiply `(z^g rem)^(k-1)` by its valuation premium; the sole emitter receives the appropriate cofactor discount. This is not the product of separately minimized forests. The exact checker uses three location states (no exception, at this label, or below) and independently recomputes the tree, in contrast to the two simultaneous return values used in discovery.

Both mass caps and valuation tails are only lower bounds. Future ordinary primes carry factors at least one, apart from the explicit active Euler-one discount; bound that by the smallest label ratio, once. For `o=ord_p(z)` the LTE inequality `p^a<=p^lev(g+1)` bounds the valuation tail, with `lev=v_p(z^o-1)` for `o>1` or zero for order one. A monotonicity check justifies dropping all higher exponents. All decisive comparisons are exact integers or rational numbers.
