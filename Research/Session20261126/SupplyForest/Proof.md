## Valuation ledger

Let `A=sigma(p^e)`, `a=v_p(sigma(r^f))`, `k=kappa(p)` as in the archived Teichmüller valuation theorem. The exact total of `a` outside `p` is `e`. For an ordinary prime its *valuation premium* is `max(1, r^f/p^(ka))`. Multiplying the universal valuation bound by these premiums **at actually active, distinct labels** is legitimate: in the exact product `N=p^((k+1)e) prod(r^f/p^(ka))` ratios above one pay their own premium, while the product of ratios below one is at least `1/U(e)` by the archived theorem. Outside labels with ratio above one may be forgotten. For high Euler exponent `f>=5` its deficit is at most `D`, and its positive premium is again allowed. For `k=4` (p=11) the stronger high Euler bound uses order 1: when `a>0`, `q>11`, `f>=11^a-1>=4a`; order 2: `q>=2p^t-1>=p^t`, `f>=5`, `a=t+j, j=v_p((f+1)/2)`, and for `j>=1`, `f>=2p^j-1>=4+4j`. This pays `p^(4t+4j)`; for `j=0` use `f>=5`. For the other permitted orders, the finite Hensel table includes every candidate of exact level `t<=e` whose least representative `x` is 1 mod 4 and not forbidden. If not the least representative then `q>p^t` and `f>=5`. At `j>=1`, the excess `f-fmin >= o*p^j-1-fmin >= j*(o*p-1-fmin)` covers `p^(4j)` by the check in the verifier. For Euler exponent one, its ratio is replaced by the separate factor `q/p^(ks)` (`s=v_p(q+1)`); its premium inside the tree is therefore set to one, never paid twice.

For each prime label `z` on the roots we check `z|A`; its required exponent is `v_z(A)`. Other labels have exactly one assigned parent and divide neither `p` nor `A`; the verifier checks the forest is acyclic and all vertices are reachable. Only if `y|sigma(z^f)` is a child activated, with exponent at least `v_y(sigma(z^f))`. All used labels have independently verified primality from deterministic 64-bit tests or frozen ECPP chains. Enumerate all compatible even exponents, disallowing `sigma(z^f)` with a forbidden divisor. With one possible Euler prime, also enumerate `z=1 mod 4`, `f=1 mod 4`; the exception can be taken at this vertex or passed to **one** active child. The same applies across roots. Discard a tail only when its lower bound exceeds the best found so far and remains monotone. For any `f`, LTE yields `p^a <= p^t(f+1)`, where `t=v_p(z^o-1)` for `o=ord_p(z)>1`, or zero for order one. Thus `z^f/[p^(kt)(f+1)^k]` is a valid tail bound. The allowed unit at an Euler-one vertex is one, so the verifier visits exponent one before using this tail bound; make this explicit in any variant. Since descendants have premiums at least one, no omitted tail can lower the minimum.

Write `v` for the all-even minimum product and `w` for the one-Euler minimum, allowing that the Euler label is outside the forest. High exponent is excluded by `p^((k+1)e) w > B U D`. An Euler of exponent one satisfies `q>=2p^s-1`. For `0<=s<s0` the verifier checks `p^((k+1)e) w (2p^s-1)>B U p^(ks)`. For all `s>=s0`, no forest label can be Euler because every eligible label satisfies `v_p(z+1)<s0`. Thus one may replace `w` by `v`; check the resulting inequalities for all `s0<=s<cut`. All remaining `s` share `Y=p^cut`, hence `q>=Q=2Y-1` and `q≡Q (mod 4Y)` (Euler `q≡1 mod 4`).

**Role-split variant (11×330 and 19×498).** First handle the high Euler case with only exponent `5,9,...` at the unique charged odd location (or with the location outside the tree); this avoids relaxing an expensive Euler power to exponent one. Next, if the Euler-one prime is active, charge its actual full ratio `z/p^(k v_p(z+1))`, rather than capping that ratio at one or treating it as exterior. Thus `p^((k+1)e) * minimum / U > B` independently rejects all such positions; all other labels use clipped ordinary premiums and the outside universal losses remain covered by `U`. Downstream products may be less than one in this recursion: its tail bound is explicitly multiplied by the minimum of one and every possible internal label ratio. Finally only the outside Euler-one case remains; every active exponent is even, and the full all-even tree may now be used from `s=0`. Default packets continue using the original one-coupon guard.

## Explicit Euler location and arithmetic rectangles

When `p=3 mod 4`, a prime `q|A` has `ord_q(p)|e+1` odd, whence the Legendre symbol `(p/q)=1`. For `q=1 mod 4`, reciprocity gives `(q/p)=1`. This is incompatible with `q=-1 mod p` for `s>0`. For `p=13` remove the certified root valuations, leaving `C=A/prod z^c`. A hypothetical `q|C` would give `q=a+Ti`, `C/q=b+Tj`, with `T=4Y, a=Q, b=C*a^{-1} mod T`. Here `a,b>0`, `a<=q<=C/b`. Modulo `T^2`, `j = -b*a^{-1} i + ((C-ab)/T)*a^{-1} mod T`. Split the entire interval `[a,C//b]` into consecutive closed bins `[l,h]`, `h=min(4l,C//b)`, next `l=h+1`; intersect both factor and cofactor ranges with their residue classes. Count all possible pairs in the overapproximating rectangle by Euclidean floor sums. All counts are zero for these packets; no lattice oracle or assumed factorization is needed. For `(13,646)` there is instead a finite small-root certificate: `n=e+1=647` is prime and coprime to `C`, so any `q|C` satisfies `q=1 mod n`. Combine that with `q=Q mod 4Y`, obtaining the class `z mod T` with `T=4Yn`. The positive cofactor satisfies `C/q=C*z^{-1} mod T`, bounding the whole search by `z<=q<=C/(C*z^{-1} mod T)`. Every consecutive closed interval carries an archived `NightMerge/LatticeLeaf.py` witness: its integer polynomial in the centered class index is a multiple of `q^m` at any divisor, while its coefficient bound is strictly below `q^m`, forcing zero. Exact polynomial division and Sturm exclude all integer roots giving divisors. The checker requires the intervals to tile the entire range, including both endpoints; no LLL implementation is used at verification time. For `13×640`, at exact `s` one has the additional ceiling `q <= B U p^(ks)/(p^((k+1)e) v)` directly from the all-even active tree (`q` is not an active label). For each band `[s1,s2]`, impose the class modulo `4 n p^s1` and the ceiling at `s2`, taking the minimum with the cofactor ceiling. This contains every real candidate, yet has only 92 small-degree polynomial certificates in eight bands. Both the exponent bands and factor-magnitude intervals must cover inclusively without gaps. Together with label exclusion this proves `q` divides neither `pA` nor any forest label.

## Private mass ledger and no double credit for q

Set `Z=p*A*prod(nonroot labels)`. For each activated label let `rem` be the odd `sigma(z^f)` part coprime to `Z` (repeated gcd division). All activated labels now have even exponent. The product of these remainders divides `N`: every `sigma(z^f)` is from a distinct component of `sigma(N)=2N`. The remainders need not be pairwise coprime: valuations **add** in that product, so no multiplicity is lost. They are coprime to `C`, labels and `p`. Thus `p^e C prod(z^f rem)` is a mass lower bound. To pay `q` separately, at most **one** active remainder can contain it and in multiplicity exactly one, since `q||N`. At such a possible emitter forget its entire `rem` (or retain a proved cofactor in a strengthened bound), not the remainders on all vertices. A remainder is incompatible if `rem<Q`, or `u=rem/q` cannot lie in `1<=u<=rem/Q`, `u=rem*Q^{-1} mod 4Y`. Small cofactor progressions are checked by exact divisibility. Every uncertain case retains the option to waive the remainder. One-emitter recursion allows either this vertex or at most one active child to use that waiver; outside emitters use no waiver. Then `N >= p^e C Q * private_tree_min`. No ordinary mass/valuation products are multiplied together without a compensating power on `N`. In the final mass DP, after the packet's finite admissible-exponent prefix, discard descendants and remainders and use `z^f` at the first tail exponent (monotone). All strict final comparisons are integer or exact rational arithmetic. Discovery scores are not proof data.

## Weighted certificate variants

For every row except the first four, apply the positive inequalities to **the same trace**, then minimize: `N^k >= M(trace)^(k-1) V(trace)`. Here `M` contains the separate mass factor `q`, `V` contains `q/p^(ks)`, and `V` carries the blanket divisor `U`. The external dependence collapses to `(q/p^s)^k >= (2-1/Y)^k`, uniformly for `s>=cut` (the function `2-p^(-s)` is increasing). Before minimization a node's full mass `z^f rem` is taken to power `k-1`; multiply by `max(1,z^f/p^(k a))`. At the unique possible q-emitter its cofactor `rem/q` is retained at its least positive residue `rem * Q^(-1) mod 4Y`, never at a heuristic typical size. Thus the root constant outside the DP is `(p^e C)^(k-1) p^((k+1)e) (Q/Y)^k / U`. The simultaneous minimum is in general **larger** than the product of separately minimized forests, as each local valuation premium depends on the same exponent used in the mass ledger. Weighted tail: `z^(f(k-1)) max(1, z^f/[p^(kt)(f+1)^k])`; use only past the monotonicity guard.

For three packets add the following rough residual cut. `n=e+1` is checked prime and coprime to `C`; every prime `r|C` has `ord_r(p)=n` and hence `r=1 mod 2n`. Exhaustively divide `C` by all integers in this class from `2n+1` up to (but not including) the given `L`. This proves every prime in `C` is at least `L`. None is Euler, and all are outside the labels and all private remainders. If `C` is nonsquare some such prime occurs to odd multiplicity in `C`, hence an extra factor ≥`L` in the unweighted mass ledger. If the residue of `C mod p` is outside the odd-order subgroup, one prime in `C` is outside the subgroup and cannot supply `p` with an even exponent, so its unused valuation premium is at least `L^2`. The two chosen primes **need not be distinct**: the factors belong to different ledgers of `N^k` and their multiplicities match the powers of those ledgers. If the single Euler-one prime is at an active label, it is outside `C` by construction too. Therefore the same `L²` for a nonmember prime in `C` can strengthen the **active Euler-one** valuation guard (19×498); this argument is not applied to an arbitrary unknown Euler inside `C`. Total additional `L` exponent is `(k-1)*[nonsquare]+2*[nonmember]`. For `(19,660)` we use `L=2500000000`; the strict tail bound is about `10^2400.30`, not `10^2401`.
