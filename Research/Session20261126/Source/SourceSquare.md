**Result.** For every ordinary prime power r^f exactly dividing N with q | sigma(r^f), ord_q(r)=f+1. Therefore f+1 divides q-1, 3 does not divide f+1, and b=v3(sigma(r^f))=0. Such a source exists because q|sigma(N) and the root is not its source. The statement covers internal and external r relative to sigma(3^e).

**Proof.** q ≥ Q=2·3^s−1. Ordinary components with positive 3-supply k=v3(sigma(t^h)) must have t=1 mod 3, k=v3(h+1) by LTE and h ≥ 3^k−1 ≥ 2k. The primes below 43 in this progression are forbidden, except 37, whose sigma(37²) has banned factor 7. In particular b≥7 is impossible since 43^(3^7−1)>B. All other suppliers except r provide e−s−b units.

For each integer R in the script, K=R². A prime t<R may cost less than K^k: give it the discount D_t=max(1, max K^k/t^(3^k−1) over k>=1 satisfying the ban). For all t≥R or k=0 discount 1 suffices. The enumeration stops when the ratio drops to at most 1, as (3^k−1)/k increases; if the geometric sum contains a ban then so do all higher levels. Distinct supplier primes are used once each, so the product of components carrying u units is ≥ M(u), the maximum over R of floor(K^u/product(D_t)). The script proves 3^e M(e−s)>B when s≤8. Hence q>f+1, since f≤bit_length(B), and q≠r. This rules out r=1 mod q (otherwise q|f+1). Thus ord_q(r)>1 divides f+1.

If this order m is proper, q | sigma(r^(m−1)), and (f+1)/m is odd and at least 3. Since sigma(r^(m−1))<3 r^(m−1)/2 and f≥3(m−1)+2, r^f>r²(2Q/3)^3. We combine this with the positive-supply base and exponent lower bound to compute the integer F≤r^f in the verifier, using r≥5 if b=0. Two independently valid inequalities are

- N ≥ 3^e Q F M(e−s−b), where the root, Euler, chosen source, and other ordinary suppliers are distinct;
- N ≥ 3^(e−b) F²: sigma(r^f) is odd, divides N, and is coprime to r. Dividing out 3^b from sigma(r^f) gives a divisor coprime to 3r, so 3^e r^f sigma(r^f)/3^b divides N. This does not purchase q or its parent component twice.

The verifier takes a maximum, not a product, of these bounds. It checks every integer s=1,...,e and b=0,...,min(6,e−s), even retaining the impossible s≤8. Minimum certified floor log10 over these windows is respectively 2523, 2863, 2924, 3280, 3442, so the supposition m<f+1 is impossible. Since 3|q+1 and ord_q(r)|q−1, necessarily 3 does not divide f+1, which implies b=0 also if r≠1 mod 3.
