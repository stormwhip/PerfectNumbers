# Index lift for an ordinary static forest (no fresh factors and no bound monotonicity assumption)

**Theorem.** Fix the first root p (preceding roots absent) and a successful ordinary `ForestReplay` certificate for positive even e. For every f≥e such that (e+1)|(f+1), no N≤10^2400 odd perfect with p^f ∥ N exists. In fact p need not have even actual exponent: in the seed proof the Euler prime is allowed either outside K or in any eligible K, so if p itself is Euler the outside-K state includes its possibility. Use only ledgers for project-allowed f in computing coverage. The restriction on f is divisibility, NOT merely f≥e.

**Proof.** Put h=e+1. Since (f+1)=th, A_e=σ(p^e) divides σ(p^f)=A_e(1+p^h+...+p^((t−1)h)). A_e is odd, gcd(A_e,p)=1 and σ(p^f)|2N. Thus the *same* p^e A_e divides N. Freeze **all** seed inputs and internal supports, including U=p A_e ∏(forest nonroot labels), h_z=v_z(A_e) for its K, and the initial p^e multiplier. Do NOT substitute A_f anywhere. The seed root labels are genuinely forced. Each activated forest emitter z≠p and its actual exponent contributes σ(z^g) to σ(N); the value is unaffected by the unknown actual exponent of p. Out-parts remove every prime of U, and the product of these out-parts divides the complementary N part: every sender is distinct, and any common outgoing prime valuations add with their true multiplicity. The initial base and labelled balances stay disjoint. If using a weighted proof, the seed emitter charges λ_q v_q(A_e) are legitimate because v_q(σ(p^f))≥v_q(A_e), and p is a distinct sender. Root p's 2-part is never charged, since A_e is odd. Every Euler possibility and every legal tail is still covered. Therefore the same seed lower bound contradicts N≤10^2400 for the larger index. Nothing about factorization or root-support overlap of A_f/A_e is used. One may even multiply the seed bound by p^(f−e), though unnecessary for successful seeds.

**Counter-boundary.** This transfer is specific to the static forest divisor ledger. It is not generally sound for arbitrary existing tree certificates rejecting branches because assumed p^e is *exact*, supplying exactly e p units, rejecting p|D, or enforcing an Euler mode from root parity; do not automatically lift other packets. An even-order seed e+1 and unknown root as Euler would need separate treatment. `FourReplay` on 13/600 also depends on the terminal survivor lookup at that exact exponent: not covered by this automatic lift. The basic ordinary `Forest*.json.gz` packets exclude the entire containing-index class as stated. References to prior terminal conditions must not be silently carried by `IndexLift.py`.

**Replay.** `python -I -S -B Research/NightLowRoute/Release/IndexLift.py ForestSmall18.json.gz` replays all rows of the listed ordinary packets, enumerates divisibility on the frozen 880 ledger, and prints direct/strictly-lifted counts and complete shards. For the seed 31/18, this proves P31_I00006, including 10 prior open exponents, by ONE fixed 152,438-byte seed; no own forest needed for 31/56,132,…,550. Other checked cases may overlap and must be merged by distinct pairs. `FamilyReplay.py` is an optional more expensive check re-evaluating with A_f; such reevaluation is not a requirement of this theorem.
