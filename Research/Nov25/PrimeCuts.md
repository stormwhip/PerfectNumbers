## Known-order Pollard p−1 pilot

A prime factor r of `Phi_h(p)` has `r=1 mod 2h` after stripping primes dividing h, for all the odd indices `h>1` used. We compared `gcd(2^M−1,C)` and `gcd(2^(2hM)−1,C)`, `M=lcm(1,…,3000)`, on rough blocks of every open pair. Both returned the same 222 proper-gcd rows. A gcd may be composite: e.g. `(19,676)` gives `10500938229205033=629611·16678454203`, both historical. Deterministic replay found 292 certified 64-bit anchor incidences for roots 7/11/13/19/31 and 31 for root 3, all previously known in discovery stores, zero for root 5. No new split in a 24-block stage-2 pilot (`B1=8000`, `B2=150000`) after historical factors were removed. Do not treat a failure to find a factor as evidence for primality or squarefreeness.

The order hypothesis comes from [Nielsen, §3](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0602485v1#S3); the factoring precedent and distinction between factors and unresolved cofactors are in [Brent et al., Cunningham numbers](https://maths-people.anu.edu.au/~brent/pub/pub200.html).

## Root-3 unit constraint

If exact `q^1` is Euler and `q+1=2·3^s k`, `s≥1`, division of `σ(N)=2N` by `3^e` and reduction mod 3 forces the product of normalized ordinary-source units to be `(2k)^−1 mod 3`: the root unit is 1, all ordinary powers are even, the normalized Euler σ is `2k`, and `2q≡1` since `q≡−1`. In particular with the chosen parameterization `k≡1` requires an odd number of unit −1 sources. A legal ordinary exponent can have unit −1 at **zero** 3-adic supply (e.g. `r=43`, `f=4`). A relaxation that grants an unknown rough prepaid component as a possible source cannot remove any live windows with this congruence alone.
