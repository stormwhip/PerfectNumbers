## Exact source diagnostics

The approach uses multiplicative constraint trees as in [Brent–Cohen–te Riele (1991)](https://maths-people.anu.edu.au/~brent/pub/pub116.html) and [Ochem–Rao (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-2012-02563-4), and builds on the existing [supplier-product ledger](../../SupplierBatch/PerfectNumbers2400BatchReport.md). Only the five blind prime-index blocks above qualify for the displayed run: trial division finds no divisor up to 100,000; `m=e+1` is prime; and [Joint](../../NightThree/Joint.py) independently closes the high Euler class and the Euler-one `s=0` case. For each remaining `s`, let `Y=3^s` and `q=2Yk−1` with `k` odd and `k mod 3` either 1 or 2. Reciprocity gives `q∤A`. The ordinary component supports therefore supply an even square multiple of `A`; the rough minimum and a lower square-kernel multiplier come from separately checked congruence/divisibility prefixes, never from treating the block as prime. The size test takes the maximum of the valuation tariff and square-kernel premiums, not their product.

- Each ordinary exact component `r^f` contributes `j=v3(f+1)` only if `r≡1 mod 3`, otherwise no valuation. Its normalized divisor sum modulo 3 is `(f+1)/3^j` for `r≡1`, by binomial expansion (the other case gives 1). After dividing the perfectness identity by `2·3^e`, the left-hand side requires the product of normalized ordinary/root sums times `k` to equal `q≡−1 mod 3`: for `k≡1`, at least one ordinary component must give the `−1` sign. For even `f` giving that sign, `f+1≥5·3^j`. **`j=0` is permitted:** for instance `43^4` has legal sigma for the root-three forbid mask and normalized unit `−1`. Ignoring this case would incorrectly remove the `s=e,k≡1` option.
- An exact source `r^f` emits Euler `q`: if `q>f+1`, `ord_q(r)>1` divides `f+1`, and an odd prime `ell≠3` dividing its order satisfies `q≡1 mod ell`. Check source-specific Euler lower bounds in each exact `(s,k mod 3)` class. For `c=v_r(A)`, `u=v3(σ(r^f))` and `R=floor(B/(3^e A))`, multiplicative divisibility yields `r^(2f−c) < R·3^u` because `A·σ(r^f)/3^u` and the exact root factor must divide `N`, and the source exponent in `N` is `f`. Test this independently of the supplier tariff so that shared factors of sigma are not charged twice.
- For internal `r|A`, the balanced numerators of [NightThree](../../NightThree/Proof.md#L11-L13) are multiples of `3^(s+u)`, not just `3^s`: their numerator terms after multiplying by the indicated power of `r` contain either `P=3^(e+1)` or `w(q+1)` with `w=σ(r^f)/q`. The second numerator cannot vanish since `v3(w(q+1))=s+u≤e`; the first is `−P mod (r−1)` and cannot vanish. Using the same size argument with `Z=3^(s+u)` gives `r^(f−c)>qZ/(max(P,q)(P/Z+4))`. Check finite parity/range boxes for `r^c|A`, keeping wide boxes rather than interpreting them as empty. Bounds are integers directed toward keeping all possible cases.

[SourceUnit](SourceUnit.py) tested the full `e=1162` window: 216 tag cells, 34 with exterior emission excluded but possible interior emission, 182 with both emission branches retained. No tag cell was excluded. In the separate [low/middle/high sample](SourceUnitSample.json), both tags in the lowest sampled `s` closed for `1108,990,970,856` by the size/tariff test, 8 out of 30 total sampled cells; none of those exact exponents was closed. At `e=1162,s=1055`, a diagnostic survivor has `c=9,f=44,u=2`, `r` around `10^11.6`; at `s=1162` there are wider survivors with `c=1,f=6,u=0`, around `10^111–10^117`. Those are necessary-condition boxes, not factors or OPNs.

[Wieferich sensitivity](WieferichPilot.py) / [receipt](WieferichPilot.json): if `r²|A=Φ_m(3)`, then `m|r−1` and `r²|3^m−1`, so `r` is a base-3 Wieferich prime. [Dorais–Klyve, JIS 14 (2011), §§4–5](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL14/Klyve/klyve3.tex) report no base-3 examples besides `11,1006003` to `970453984500000`; neither divides the sampled `A`. Applying that published computational cutoff to `c≥2` deletes the first `e=1162,s=1055` box (`c=9`) but a looser `c=17` box above the cutoff takes its place. No previously viable internal cell is removed (8/8 and 4/4 viable in the `1162` and `1108` samples); this literature-dependent sensitivity is deliberately not used in the standalone receipts or proof ledger.

## Reproduction

From repository root (standard library only):

```text
python -I -S -B Research/NightThree/Verify.py Batch1
python -I -S -B Research/Nov25/three/TestSourceUnit.py
python -I -S -B Research/Nov25/three/VerifyReceipts.py
python -I -S -B Research/Nov25/three/SourceUnit.py --only 1162
```

The [receipt checker](VerifyReceipts.py) checks ECM input/count/unique seeds/proper divisor evidence from the saved logs and recomputes the small source sample. It reports `192` curves and `8` excluded sampled `(s,tag)` cells; it does not rerun ECM or certify a complete remaining actual.
