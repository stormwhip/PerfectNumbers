- The unconditioned [terminal LP pilot](TerminalPilot.py) for 10 low root-5 targets, given known root factors, reached numerical floors of 1439–1762, largest at e=48. [Exact-component terminal pilot](StateTerminalPilot.py) raised selected first unresolved archived endpoints to 2630.722 at e=30, 3035.675 at e=40 and 2833.659 at e=42, but an endpoint is not a complete tree. e=28 remained 2285.390. At e=48, fixing the largest root factor's exponent f=10,12,16,18 is still insufficient (1929.576, 2078.003, 2211.325, 2301.342); f=22 gave diagnostic 2501.014. [Bounded hybrid discovery](HybridPilot.py) found six LP leaf candidates at e=70, then hit its time limit (219 visits, six LPs). e=48 similarly hit its time limit (80 visits, seven LP leaves scored 2849.442–3078.052). Numerical feasibility/infeasibility and budget exits never enter the ledger.
- [Transfer](TerminalTransfer.py) and [generator](FindTransfer.py) tested the root-5 late Euler terminal mechanism at the root-3 first surviving external Euler-one windows. Using the root-3 canonical mask and rough root support, the dyadic floors for `(e,s0)` were `(1162,1055)` 1624.271700; `(1108,928)` 1512.154043; `(990,787)` 1332.292172; `(970,763)` 1301.758747; `(856,629)` 1129.054972. Once root 3 contributes an upper abundancy of 3/2, the relaxation charges only about 11.6 decimal orders above the included root component, root sigma and Euler factor. Artificially forbidding more small primes can raise the floor but was not proved for these windows.
