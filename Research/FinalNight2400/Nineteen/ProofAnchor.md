# First-pivot values 18 and 20 excluded by an exact ordinary anchor

Assume odd perfect N≤10^2400, v_19(N)=618. With t=2477 this excludes both exact f=v_t(N)=18 and 20, including all Euler exponents. See [compulsory-credit theorem](Proof618Credit.md) for notation and [the square overlap lemma](Proof618Square.md). No support mask or other key used. Command:

    python -I -S -B Research/FinalNight2400/Nineteen/VerifyAnchor.py

A sealed factor input authenticates all prime divisors of C0=σ(t^f) stripped of 19. Set z to the largest, respectively 151297224100319027205239 and 380135001457350914720133393511759489. Set X0=19^618 t^f, A=σ(19^618)/t, T=t^(f−1). As in the earlier proof q≡2T−1 mod 4T and q∤C0, so z is ordinary; v_z(C0)=1. Enumerate the even exponents h=v_z(N)≥2 under X0 A C0 z^(h−1)≤B. For X=X0 z^h, update the ordinary 19 supply to c=618−v_19(σ(t^f)σ(z^h)) still needed. Reject negative c, impossibility of t supply from the ordinary row, and X D>B, where D=A H with H=(C0/z)·Eo and Eo=σ(z^h) stripped of 19,t. All are the odd compulsory emissions outside the three fixed bases. This leaves h=2,4,...,14 and 2,4,...,10 respectively.

Available credit roots are all prime factors of C0 except z, and when h=2, also certified factors of σ(z²), after excluding the three fixed bases. The factor pack checks the *complete* support of σ(z²); even a subset would suffice. For each y outside the Euler row, we may claim E_y=min_{g positive even, y^g≤B} y^g/19^(2v_19(σ(y^g))); this is conservative even if its valuation in D is larger. The size credit is y when v_y(D) is odd and 1 otherwise. The largest possible individual supply is M_G. Use the product of credits only for distinct bases. Before fixing s=v_19(q+1), only credit roots lying in C0 or not congruent to q modulo 4T are known ordinary. The basic price with this *safe* set is valid for all s; at any successful s the exact simple price (XQ_s)^d p^(n(c−s)) is decreasing, so also excludes all lower values. No floating point is used.

For s>0, q∤A by reciprocity and q∤C0. Even if q divides Eo, the lower bound X0 A C0 Q_s still applies. Otherwise the usual progression quotient test proves q∤Eo. Then q is external to D and all credit roots are ordinary. If this cofactor exclusion holds at s it also holds for larger s: the allowed q class mod 4T·19^(s+1) is contained in that mod 4T·19^s. Thus the increasing size test X D Q_s F>B may terminate an s loop. The sharper price and the external q-supplier bound now follow the credit proof, replacing C by H, which need not be prime or squarefree. To avoid the possibility of shared factors between A and H, use M=max(A,H) and the square-overlap gain **only** when gcd(A,H)=1. Otherwise take M=D and do not use that gain. Size and price are checked separately on u≤M_G and u>M_G so the labelled q-supplier is never charged as an independent compulsory prime. q^a is handled by the same q² even-length-exponent atom as in the previous proof.

Integer comparison detail: a positive numerator weight of log2(x) uses floor(K log2 x)=bit_length(x^K)−1 (K=512), while a denominator is rounded up; upper thresholds K·bit_length(floor(B^d U/V)+1), and likewise for 3B, safely overestimate. A strict comparison after this relaxation is sufficient. Note that binary search on the initial *rounded* comparison is safe without any rounded monotonicity assumption: every interval discarded as excluded starts below a successful comparison where the **exact** price is monotone. For each unpruned s, the size test is monotone in u and the price test at the largest remaining u excludes all lower ones. The verifier checks the residual set is empty for each f. In union with the other accepted/candidate work, the first pivot now has only eight evens 2..16 outstanding.
