# The final f=2 first-pivot tree at exact (19,618)

The assumptions are N odd perfect, N≤10^2400, v_19(N)=618 and v_2477(N)=2, with no support mask. The exact square σ(2477²)=19·323053 forces 323053; the proved q congruence makes it ineligible for Euler. A complete tree of subsequent exact non-Euler powers is supplied in Solved2.json (SHA-256 8ae4fcd6b6dbe36a85ffc7f7883c7095e5504880835d5f0f1a7f87a6aa03c2b1). Its split candidates are merely hints: all exponents compatible with actual N are independently generated up to the full monotone tail, and missing edges are acceptable only if the verified leaf arithmetic already excludes them. The packet has 197 proposed split nodes across 140 distinct possible split primes, with depth indices 0 through 7. Unused hints never introduce a proof assumption. The branch 323053², for instance, forces 2675988817; its fourth power yields 24250282152605596121 and 2114561792943717421, enabling efficient exact splits. Older discovery trees and factorizations without certificates are not needed.

All geometric, legal, incoming-valuation and product lower bounds are those of [ProofNested.md](ProofNested.md) and [ProofSmallSix.md](ProofSmallSix.md). The two rigorous nondivisibility tests at u≥185 and squared-overlap tests at u≥98 are replayed from their polynomial witnesses, just as for f=6 and 4. As always, the Euler prime may have arbitrarily high permitted exponent; its residual q² atom is priced without overrestricting it to prime bases. Distinct ordinary extraction, compulsory-credit bounds, conditional repayment and the two increasing/decreasing inequalities are unchanged.

The default entry optimizes two exact computations, with no changes to mathematical comparisons:

* For b=512 and n=m·2^k+δ (0≤δ<2^k), n^b/2^(kb) belongs to [m^b,(m+1)^b). If the lower bit index of m^b and the bit index of (m+1)^b−1 agree then floor(b log2 n) is kb plus that common integer. Otherwise take 64 leading bits, then fall back to exact n^b. Applying this to positive n gives exactly the original integer function; no approximate boundary can turn into a proof.
* For a compulsory odd prime z, h even, compute the exact p=19 emission valuation using o=ord_p(z)∈{1,3,9} whenever a positive supply is possible. If o=1 then u=v_19(h+1); if o>1 divides h+1 then u=v_19(z^o−1)+v_19(h+1); else u=0. For all even h through z^h≤B this gives precisely the old net minimum and max supply. The condition z=19 is ineligible for credits because it is a fixed row. Integer cross multiplication compares the rational minima.
