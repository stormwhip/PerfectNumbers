# Reduced 19^358 grid with an exterior supplier

Extend [the initial proof](Proof.md), with the identical primality, source, LTE and price computations. In each weak cell consider every j-axis Euler allocation b and every a on the p-axis (notation here: m=T p^a s^b), with V=p^(2cp)s^(10cs). Q=2m−1. If X Q V/(p^(2a)s^(10b))>BU the initial price excludes it. When a,b are positive both corresponding interior blocks are excluded by reciprocity; on the coordinate axes use the exact cofactor congruence. The third block is always exterior as already proved. Then either XDQ>B or an ordinary emitter y^h emits q. Put P,S for its target supply products, and H=max_l Z_l ∏_{k≠l}gcd(Z_l,Z_k) for the three stripped fixed blocks. For any particular prime y, choose the block attaining maximal v_y(Z_l); this H upper bounds the entire pre-paid y-adic part of D, even if blocks overlap. The mandatory lower bound gives PS≤floor(3BH/(2XDQ²)); the complementary price gives 3NU>2XQ² V/(p^(2a)s^(10b) P S⁹). For fixed S=s^v choose the maximal P=p^u under the first limit and remaining supply caps. Maximize over all v, as implemented in [Discrete](Discrete.py). This proves the contradiction for every possible emitter, not merely the cheapest separate suppliers.

For speed the code omits a prefix of a values by monotonicity of the initial price: (2Kz−1)/z² is nonincreasing for z≥1, K=T s^b≥1. After a successful source test when a,b>0, larger a can be skipped: both blocks stay exterior by reciprocity, Q²/(p^a)²=(2T s^b−p^(−a))² increases, while the permitted P,S domain shrinks. A break after the mandatory test likewise requires permanent externality by either reciprocity or size for each block. Thus neither early stop extrapolates an accidental cofactor test. A second, slower grid calculation used no source-monotonic pruning and only the continuous upper bound P S⁹≤min(limit·min(limit,s^(cs−b))^8, p^(cp−a)s^(9(cs−b))); it reproduced the same 645 exclusions. The explicit projection: all 21 i values 56,58,…,96 and 17 j values 48,50,…,80 excluded; the exact remaining 323 joint cells (not all of their rectangular hull) are returned by check(). This is a conditional improvement of 106 cells over the first packet, not new closure credit for the key.
