## Last odd valuation D¹

[VerifyDOne](VerifyDOne.py) replays a [352-node finite tree](D1Scout.json) and its [41 new ECPP certificates](D1Primes.json). It uses only stdlib and pure-integer historical kernels; PARI and the author factor bank generated data but are not used by proof replay. At the prefix 19^46 q^6 D¹ price weights 2,4 on (19,q), caps (46,5); Euler is fixed. Start with the net-price identity and unique per-base correction in [the T⁶ proof](../Ten/ProofSix.md). Every explicit base is a certified prime divisor of a compulsory geometric sum.

At each recursive node let K be the assigned exact mass, S the product of all odd σ-parts, Y=S/gcd(S,K). Require gcd(Y,K)=1 or contradictions from overflow. All unassigned primes are ordinary. For every distinct compulsory r in the known set, start at the least even f≥v_r(Y). Using the current finite set of proven absent primes, increase f to the first even value whose geometric sum avoids every absent prime. Multiplicativity and monotonicity give an abundancy lower bound I(K)·∏I(r^f). If >2 the node closes; otherwise, for each tested distinct unassigned candidate u not among the known mandatory factors, if this lower bound times I(u²)>2, then u cannot divide N. Add all such proven bans and iterate to stability. No prime is assumed absent from the original canonical family. For example the initial D¹ factors already make 5,7,13 impossible, and after raising the incoming minima, 23 as well.

For a compulsory r define its net gain as E_r times the minimum of r^f divided by the weighted target supplies in σ(r^f), across even f≥threshold avoiding banned outputs and not individually exceeding a cap. All f are checked until the raw bound r^f/M passes the current best (M is the full weighted demand of the original prefix). Split on one such r when the product of all mandatory net gains still fails to exceed B. Each exact exponent replaces that r's minimum by its actual net factor, and verified fresh output primes may be introduced. Recompute valuations from the new S and maintain a set, so a factor appearing from several parents is counted once, with its true compulsory valuation in Y. Already assigned bases are not admitted as new gains. Exhaustive tails use nondecreasing allowed minima and K r^f alone as alternatives. [Verifier](VerifyDOne.py) regenerates the entire child domain, independently checks every forced or banned base, confirms coverage including tails, and rejects any open leaf. Counts: 140 splits, 201 strict net-price terminals, 11 strict abundancy terminals. Final replay 10.882 s exit 0. The tree metadata is not relied on for arithmetic.
