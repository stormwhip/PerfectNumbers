At a fixed ordinary extension P with 1093^1 already assigned, put X=∏z^h, S=σ(X)/2, D=S/gcd(S,X), a=v3(X)−v3(S) and c=v547(X)−v547(S). All unassigned primes are ordinary. For each r^f exactly dividing N outside X, let u=v3(σ(r^f)), v=v547(σ(r^f)), and for a chosen integer λ≥1 set g(r,f)=r^f/(λ^u 547^(2v)). The actual sums of u,v are exactly a,c. In particular

    N = X·λ^a·547^(2c) · ∏g(r,f).

For fixed λ, first compute all candidate *deficits* 1/g>1 over every unassigned odd prime r≥5, admissible even f, u≤6 and v≤281. Construct a superset by enumerating even f until 5^f≥547^(2·281)λ^6. If r≡1 mod3, then u=v3(f+1), else u=0 (since f even and r≠3). If r≡1 mod547, v=v547(f+1); else a nontrivial order divides gcd(546,f+1), and LTE gives v=j+v547(f+1), where j is the depth of r in the simple-root lift for that order. For the v=0 option enumerate every positive r below the corresponding cutoff. For the other options generate every lifted progression and every positive representative r^f<547^(2v)λ^min(6,v3(f+1)), then recompute both actual valuations. If r is not 1 mod3, it can only be a deficit when r^f<547^(2v); incorrect-progressions rejection is thereby avoided. Remove only invalid candidates by proper trial factors, failed Fermat tests, fixed or banned bases, Euler parity, or forbidden σ emissions. At a particular prefix also reject over-cap r, and exact mass X·lcm(D,r^f)>B. Tables need not prove primality of all retained candidates: composites only relax the lower bound.

Select distinct authenticated labelled primes r|D, called L. For every unlabelled base retain possible deficit factors indexed by u. Group each r once, retaining the best benefit per u, and compute a knapsack maximum U over total 3-weight ≤a (rather than multiplying every possibly mutually incompatible exception). An overestimate is harmless, as long as each actual unlabelled row with 1/g>1 is available in the corresponding group and its total u cannot exceed a. A labelled r is explicitly **excluded from this discount table**. For each r∈L compute the exact minimum G_r of g(r,f) over feasible even f≥v_r(D), excluding a forbidden emitted factor, over-cap 3 or 547, or any emission of the already-saturated exact 1093. The scan stops if its bare power exceeds B/X, or once r^f/(λ^a 547^(2c)) already exceeds the best seen (safe for every larger f). Lack of any admissible f is itself impossible. Therefore

    N ≥ X·λ^a·547^(2c)·(∏_{r∈L}G_r)/U.
