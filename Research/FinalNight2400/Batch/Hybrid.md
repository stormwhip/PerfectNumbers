# Subprefixes of the 11^396 two-target price

Assume the canonical (11,396) class and an exact prefix containing 11^396 and t^f for t=2383, followed by distinct compulsory primes at exact exponents. Let X be their exact product, S the product of odd parts of the fixed sigma emissions, D=S/gcd(S,X), and c,d the unsupplied units at 11,t. Discard illegal prefixes before invoking the following price. The previously verified [weight (4,2) proof](Proof.md) works on any subset of unfixed atoms; at no stage are compulsory factors multiplied by a price for those same factors. Write U for the uniform loss computed at the original maximal caps. If a prefix already fixes Euler, XV>BU suffices, with V=11^(4c)t^(2d). Otherwise split the Euler row into linear q and ordinary-length q². For i,j supplied by q+1, m=11^i t^j, Q=2m−1, N U ≥ X Q V/(11^(4i)t^(2j)). The checker first applies the original cap lemma to *all* stripped blocks. For block of base 11 or t the corresponding linear supply must vanish by reciprocity if q divides that block; for other blocks no such deletion is made. If q lies outside, XDQ≤B. All other windows are enumerated if the coarse cap is insufficient.

For an outside q, designate one ordinary y^h which emits q. It emits target powers P,T; necessity is P T≤floor(3BH/(2XDQ²)) where H=max_l Z_l·product_{j≠l}gcd(Z_l,Z_j) on the stripped fixed blocks. For the chosen y take l maximizing its multiplicity in a block: all overlapping y-powers elsewhere are bounded by the respective gcds. Independently price the other atoms to obtain 3NU>2X Q² V/(11^(4i)t^(2j)P³T). Enumerate permissible T=t^v and choose the greatest P=11^u allowed by the product cap and the residual u cap. Interior q is ruled out by reciprocity or individual cofactor progression for *every* block before this source is invoked. The strict price test rejects the entire grid, including high Euler valuations. Empty grids are checked arithmetically, not by a solver. [Implementation](Prefix.py).

[Hybrid verifier](Hybrid.py) recomputes every compatible exact child under its certified mandatory prime, rejects omitted siblings, calls that theorem on the exact prefix at price nodes, and checks two exact recurrences at forest nodes. The raw exponent loop stops only after a strict monotone budget tail. The packet does not impose any additional support assumption. Only the four claimed first exponents are covered here, not a lift to other exact components.
