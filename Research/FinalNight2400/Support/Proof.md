# Larger-prime support restrictions, independent of exact root exponent

Assume N odd perfect, N≤10^2400, 3,5,19,31 absent. Then the support meets {53,59}; if 53 is absent, 59 divides N and at least two of {41,73,137} divide N. There is no support-mask or frontier assumption. This does not close a canonical shard.

**Dependencies.** At baseline 1447d41b806f43ec5775e0cef7eaa7739ab67388, the historical [support deductions](../../FinalSprint/Support/README.md) imply that 7,11,13,17,23,29,37,47 all divide N. The [old whole-domain pricing proof](../../FinalSprint/Support/GlobalVerify.py) establishes the forbidden-47 cut; the new calculation uses the same sparse dual of the looser model in [Side1931](../../FinalSprint/Support/Side1931.json). New deductions concern primes beyond 47, not another proof of the earlier deductions. We do not import a target-exponent bound, nor treat a sampled list of survivors as the entire domain.

**Repricing.** Reconstruct the historical basis with 3,5,19,31 forbidden; zero the duals of explicit support group rows so the lower bound can be re-minimized under additional support conditions. Audit.replay certifies all anonymous and unbounded flow variables, as well as all explicit and tail columns, with outward log bounds. From its lower bound subtract the old group minima min(0,min cost(column)). The remainder includes the (possibly negative) anonymous Euler saving and the constant dual term, unchanged. For a new set J of forbidden primes the corresponding groups can only choose absence (reduced cost zero); for other groups delete each exact exponent emitting a factor in J. An unbounded odd-exponent tail is deleted only if q+1 itself emits that factor; for even tails never delete on the strength of one exponent. Every real factorization maps to a surviving column in each active group. For the eight required primes, absence is not an option. Add the minimal available even (or absent) reduced cost for each group and then the single largest possible saving from replacing it by an odd option. At most one actual component is Euler; allowing one explicit saving on top of the anonymous saving only relaxes the lower bound. Thus the resulting integer bound does not exceed the true log-volume in the model scale. The scale divisor is rounded UP; a floor strictly above 2400 gives a contradiction.

For J={53,59}, {53,41,73}, {53,41,137}, {53,73,137}, the four certified decimal floors are respectively 2403,2411,2412,2410. The last three cuts rule out each absent pair in {41,73,137} when 53 is absent. Replay with Python -I -S -B -X int_max_str_digits=0 and [Verify.py](Verify.py). This overlay relies on the historical global supports and their whole-domain relaxation proof; it does not establish a full-history cold replay.
