# (13,388): disjoint cubic and quintic supply lengths

These are two of the [nine baseline open first fibers](../../Night2400/Receipts/Review11x462And13x388.md), not all. No forbidden support is needed. Let p=13 and let X,A,C,M,D be the fixed mass, two geometric sums, remaining target demand product and stripped outgoing mass as in the [square-base proof](../Root7Joint/Proof.md), with c_p,c_t recomputed by division. The prefix Euler prime is not fixed. Factor q^a=q(q²)^((a−1)/2) and treat the even geometric atom on q² separately from q+1 exactly as there.

Give p and t respective integer weights 2,4. For an even-index atom (b,h), an incoming target l at b≡1 mod l requires l^u|h+1; otherwise a positive supply at order o requires o·l^k|h+1, u=z+k, z=v_l(b^o−1), k=v_l(h+1). The odd prime supports of these minimal lengths for p and t are disjoint: they divide powers of 3·13 and of 5·389·911·t, respectively. Thus h≥(d_p−1)+(d_t−1), where the two required lengths d_i divide h+1 and are coprime. For either target, order one costs no deficit, and the LTE surcharge beyond o−1 costs no deficit, by 3^(l−1)≥l^weight. The unit-order budget is at least (l−1)u; the surcharge budget is at least (l−1)k. Remaining base-order losses at depth z are l^(weight*z)/b^(o−1); enumerate all odd orders dividing odd(l−1) and all lift depths ≤c_l. If o−1≥weight then a base at least l^z cannot lose; enumerate all primitive roots by generator and simple-root Hensel lifting. For high orders where the total demand already fits at b=3 discard the order; otherwise the checker may enumerate all odd b below the exact cost threshold instead. For each base/target take the maximum loss, at least 1. Products U over both targets give N≥XqV/U where V=p^(2(c_p−i))t^(4(c_t−j)), i,j the valuations of q+1. This applies to any subset of the atoms, using the associated supplies only. The primality filter is one-sided: accept b if it passes base-2 Fermat OR is a square of an odd number 1 mod 4 passing base-2 Fermat; reject a multiple of small prime s only if neither s nor s². Thus no actual ordinary prime or actual Euler square is removed. This sharply improves the loss at *integer* weights without assuming pseudoprimes prime.

Otherwise exclude q from each stripped fixed sigma block by size or by testing the sole positive cofactor congruence when its permitted interval is shorter than 4m. Once q is external, try XDQ>B. Else an ordinary component r^h, r not fixed, must emit q. For its supplies u,v at p,t, 3r^h>2Q p^u t^v. Pricing all other atoms and retaining this component gives 3NU>2XQ²V/(p^u t^(3v)); mandatory mass gives 3NH>2XDQ²p^u t^v, where H bounds the r-part of D. Then r lies in a nontrivial order root class modulo l^(u0−k0), also for any larger actual supply. Root classes and unique possible cofactor representatives below the modulus are exhaustively tested if modulus²>Z. For large order o one may instead test all r with Xr^(o−1)≤B when this domain is small enough; otherwise no exclusion is taken. For coprime blocks H is at most the larger non-eliminated block, else the product suffices. These two tests are alternative inequalities, not multiplied.
