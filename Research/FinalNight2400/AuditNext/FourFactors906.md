# Four distinct prime factors necessary without assuming A squarefree, at high Euler or linear zero supply

Under canonical v₇(N)=906, let A=Φ907(7) and q^a be Euler. If a≥5 or v₇(q+1)=0, then **A must have at least four distinct prime factors**. In these same cases it has at most seven ordinary prime factors by [the gain lemma](Gain906.md). No squarefreeness, complete factorization, limit on multiplicities, or significant-digit comparison is presumed. This sharpens the lower factor count of three from [Supply906](Supply906.md).

Proof. If A had ≤3 distinct primes, A³≢1 mod49 guarantees some actual prime of A has 7-depth ≤1 (regardless of its multiplicity), therefore supplies ≤5 to σ(N). If this base is Euler with exponent ≥5, quadratic reciprocity from ord_q(7)=907 restricts its order mod 7 to 1 or 3, so the same estimate applies. If Euler is linear with zero supply it is immediately harmless. Every remaining A-prime supplies u≤258 ([single-divisor lemma](Divisor906.md)). Zero-pad to two other potential primes. Call one of these high if u≥164. For such a base the [square-divisor lemma](Repeated906.md) forces v_r(A)=1. Its true exponent is at least two (an Euler component of exponent one in the present cases cannot have positive 7 supply), so at least one full additional r must be purchased beyond A. Moreover r>10^7·7^(u−4) by the verified CRT screening used by [the gain lemma](Gain906.md). These extra independent r's do not overlap external suppliers.

For h high labels, the unpaid outside supply is at least 906−5−Σu. Cost from disjoint exterior components is ≥7^(2(906−5−Σu))/U with U≤579125. Multiply by mandatory XA and the above r premiums. Within each high/low category the resulting lower bound is smallest with u at its maximum: an additional supply unit saves 49 in the required exterior price, costing at most a factor 7 in the separate high premium. Thus substitute h values 258, the other 2−h values 163. For h=0,1,2 respectively, exact lower bounds have decimal floors **2497,2558,2619**, all larger than 2400. Consequently ≤3 distinct factors is impossible. [Verifier](FourFactors906.py) replays both complete lattice tables and the exact gain/price tests. Scope does not include an arbitrary nonzero-s exterior linear Euler allocation; this is still a conditional theorem and not a global exclusion of 7^906.
