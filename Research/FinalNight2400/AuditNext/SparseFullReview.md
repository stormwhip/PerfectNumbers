# Independent finite-pattern verification: ≥3 distinct prime factors required in Φ907(7)

**Accepted structural theorem.** For a putative odd perfect N≤10^2400 with v₇(N)=906 in its canonical 19-absent branch, A=(7^907−1)/6 cannot have at most two distinct prime factors, **regardless of their multiplicities and of the Euler prime/exponent**. This does not factor A unconditionally or eliminate (7,906). Prior independently checked refinements: high Euler/zero supply requires ≥4 distinct factors, and if A is squarefree it requires ≥5. Proof and scope at [ThreeFactors.md](../SparseSeven/ThreeFactors.md).

[Joint author's exact last-stage verifier](../SparseSeven/VerifyThreeFactors.py) independently replayed all sparse candidate enumeration in **285.513 s**, no LLL oracle or outside service. A [second implementation](SparsePatterns.py), no imports from author arithmetic, ran **485.908 s**. It exhaustively lifted by testing all 7 digits (not the author's derivative), used integer Newton roots and directly intersected conservative lower bounds on component mass with r^i b^j=A. If gcd(i,j)>1 the input A is a perfect power, which exact root tests rule out. For d=jh−ig the X-free size r^h b^g is bounded from below via raising to j if d≥0, to i otherwise; the negative exponent is never turned into an incorrect lower bound. The emitter's additional 7-inflow and σ-supply mass are applied to the same fixed component, not paid on a second copy.
