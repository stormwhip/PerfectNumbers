# (7,906): factor-free 7-adic supply cap on every ordinary divisor of Φ907(7)

Let A=(7^907−1)/6 and 7^906∥N, where N is odd perfect and N≤10^2400. For every ordinary exact component r^h∥N with r|A, **v₇(σ(r^h))≤258**. Equivalently any ordinary component supplying ≥259 units is exterior to A, so if combined with another compulsory divisor H it can overlap AH only in H. This improves on the [historic square-divisibility exclusion](../../Session20261126/Anonymous/AnonymousGuard.md): that result forbids only r²|A at lift depth 183, and allows r|A. The two independent restrictions can be used together, not as repeated charges. No claim that sufficiently many such suppliers must exist; the root key remains open.

**Proof.** A prime r|A has ord_r(7)=907, whence r≡1 modulo 1814. Indeed 907 is prime, gcd(A,7·1814)=1 and if r|6 then r∤A. To attain u≥259, r cannot be 1 mod 7 because that would imply u=v₇(h+1)≤4 (3^(7^5−1)>B). The only other odd order at 7 is 3. LTE for the odd length h+1 then gives v₇(r³−1)≥u−4≥255 and r is a nontrivial cubic root modulo 7^255. The two simple roots 2,4 mod 7 lift uniquely. CRT with 1 mod 1814 gives two classes for r modulo M=1814·7^255. Include the two inverse classes A/r mod M as well. Either r or A/r is a divisor b≤sqrt(A) in this set (all least representatives >1). It suffices to exclude b throughout four full progressions.

Let b=R+Mx, x≥0; check R∤A at x=0. For x>0 choose c≡R M⁻¹ mod A; b|(x+c). On every divisor band lo≤b≤hi choose integers k,n from the [certificate](Divisor906.json.gz), and x≤X=floor((hi−R)/M). For l=0,…,n−1 let j=min(l,k). Then x^(l−j)(x+c)^j A^(k−j) is divisible by b^k. Two certified integral combinations of these polynomials have coefficient absolute sums weighted by X^d strictly below lo^k. Their evaluations at any candidate must vanish exactly. Their reductions mod the proved prime 65537 have polynomial gcd one, so no common integer root exists. The checker requires every nonempty class-band pair and a contiguous partition through isqrt(A). It never assumes LLL output quality or probabilistic primality.
